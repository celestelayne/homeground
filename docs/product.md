# HomeGround Product

## What is HomeGround?

HomeGround helps people build a complete picture around a property they're considering.

You find the property. HomeGround helps you understand the place around it.

## Who is the HomeGround ICP?

International buyers considering property in France, particularly second-home buyers and buyers researching unfamiliar areas.

## What is the HomeGround experience?

HomeGround starts with a property.

From there, users can research its location, understand relevant evidence, explore what is around it, and add their own context as they learn more or visit in person.

**Web:** research properties from afar, explore their surroundings, save context, and compare options.

**Mobile:** take that research with you and add places, notes, photos, and observations while exploring an area.

## Product Boundary

HomeGround is not a property search portal or a general-purpose maps app.

The property remains the anchor.

**Property first. Context around it.**
