# ADR-004: Deterministic Evidence

## Decision

AI cannot generate or substitute HomeGround geographic evidence.

Evidence must come from defined data sources and reproducible methodology.

## Why

HomeGround evidence needs to be traceable and reproducible.

Language models can produce plausible answers without establishing that those answers are geographically or methodologically correct.

## Consequence

When HomeGround can determine something from authoritative data or deterministic calculation, it should do so.

AI may eventually help explain or interact with structured evidence, but it must not manufacture the underlying evidence.

If the required data or methodology is unavailable or unresolved, the result remains Unknown.
